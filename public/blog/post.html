How to choose the right drum size for your home

The drum you buy should match how you use it. Here is how to pick the right size.

For water storage

Most homes use between 40L and 60L per person per day. A family of four using 200L per day needs the 1000L drum for 5 days of backup. For smaller households, 170L or 250L drums work.

For grain or dry goods

Air-tight drums preserve grain for months. 170L drums hold roughly 100 kg of maize — good for a season of small consumption.

For oil and liquids

Metal drums are safer for flammable liquids. Plastic works for cooking oil and non-flammable fluids.
