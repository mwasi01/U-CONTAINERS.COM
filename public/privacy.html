Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

What we collect

  • Account details: name, email, phone — when you register or place an order.
  • Order details: products, prices, delivery addresses, timestamps.
  • Messages: anything you send via our contact form or WhatsApp.
  • Technical data: IP address and browser info via our CDN provider for security.

How we use it

  • To process and deliver your orders.
  • To contact you about your order.
  • To respond to messages you send us.
  • To detect and prevent fraud and abuse.

Who we share it with

We share data only with the service providers required to run the business: Cloudflare (hosting), our delivery riders (name, phone, address only), and M-Pesa (payment). We do not sell your data.

Your rights

Contact nopgiveup@gmail.com to request a copy of your data or ask for deletion. We action requests within 30 days.

Cookies

We use localStorage to remember your cart and login session. No tracking cookies. No third-party analytics.
