Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services

What areas do you deliver to?

We offer free delivery within Nairobi for orders above KES 10,000. For areas outside Nairobi, we charge delivery fees based on distance. Contact us for a specific quote for your location.

How long does installation take?

Most installations take 2-4 hours depending on the complexity. Simple drum setups can be completed in under an hour, while comprehensive water storage systems may take half a day.

Do you offer emergency repair services?

Yes, we offer 24/7 emergency repair services for commercial clients and premium package subscribers. Standard clients can access emergency services at an additional fee.

Can I get a service package for my farm workers?

Absolutely! We offer customized training packages for farm workers. Contact us to discuss your specific training needs and we'll create a package that fits your requirements and budget.

What's included in the maintenance service?

Our maintenance service includes inspection, cleaning, lubrication, adjustment, and minor repairs. We also provide a detailed report on the condition of your equipment and recommendations for any necessary repairs or replacements.
