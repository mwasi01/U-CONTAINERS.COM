Our Story & Commitment

Since 2019, we've been providing quality drums, containers, and agricultural solutions to Kenyan homes and businesses with persistence and dedication.

Who We Are

NEVER GIVE UP ENTERPRISE is a Kenyan-owned business specializing in high-quality storage containers, energy-efficient jikos, and durable farming tools. Founded in 2019, we have grown from a small local supplier to a trusted name across Kenya.

Our name reflects our philosophy - we never give up on providing the best quality products and exceptional service to our customers. Every product we sell is carefully selected and tested to ensure it meets our high standards.

Based in Kikuyu, we serve customers across Kenya, from individual homeowners to large agricultural businesses and industrial facilities. Our commitment to quality and customer satisfaction has earned us a reputation as one of the most reliable suppliers in the industry.

Our Mission & Vision

Guiding principles that drive our business forward

Our Mission

To provide high-quality, durable, and affordable storage and agricultural solutions that meet the evolving needs of Kenyan homes and businesses, while maintaining exceptional customer service and building lasting relationships.

  • Provide quality products at competitive prices
  • Ensure customer satisfaction through excellent service
  • Support local communities and businesses

Our Vision

To become Kenya's leading supplier of storage containers and agricultural tools, recognized for our quality products, reliability, and commitment to customer success, while expanding our reach to serve more communities across East Africa.

  • Be the market leader in storage solutions
  • Expand our product range and services
  • Promote sustainable agriculture practices
