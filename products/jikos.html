Jiko Maintenance Tips

Proper care extends the life of your jiko and ensures optimal performance. Follow these simple tips:

Regular Cleaning

Remove ash after each use to maintain proper air flow and efficiency.

Check Ceramic Liner

Inspect the ceramic liner regularly for cracks and replace when necessary.

Store Properly

Keep your jiko in a dry place when not in use to prevent rust and damage.

Use Quality Charcoal

Good quality charcoal burns cleaner and produces less ash, extending jiko life.
