Tool Care & Maintenance

Proper care extends the life of your farming tools and ensures they work efficiently for years.

1

Clean After Use

Remove soil and debris from tools after each use to prevent rust and corrosion.

2

Sharpen Regularly

Keep cutting edges sharp for efficient work and reduced physical effort.

3

Oil Metal Parts

Apply light oil to metal parts to prevent rust, especially during rainy seasons.

4

Store Properly

Store tools in a dry place, hanging if possible to prevent damage to edges.
